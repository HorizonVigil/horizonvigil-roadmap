# V2 — Observability and Incident Management

## Order

1. OTLP ingestion, identity, quotas, buffering and retention
2. Metrics and infrastructure monitoring
3. Logs, traces, APM, RUM, synthetics, profiling and topology
4. Alerting, anomaly/causal analysis, SLOs, on-call and incidents

Telemetry must be tenant-scoped at ingestion. High cardinality, sensitive fields, sampling, retention and usage cost are product controls rather than operational afterthoughts.
